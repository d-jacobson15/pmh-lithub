PMH LitHub

At Parramatta Marist High School, we are committed to ensuring that every student can:

  • Comprehend texts to acquire information.
  • Deconstruct academic tasks to identify the requisite knowledge and skills.
  • Express knowledge clearly using an appropriate text type.

To do this, teachers need a toolkit of strategies to:

  • Expose students to subject-specific texts and teach strategies to navigate them.
  • Demonstrate how to deconstruct academic tasks to identify their key requirements.
  • Scaffold the construction of effective subject-specific responses.

At PMH, we understand literacy as a combination of:

Foundational Literacy

Foundational literacy encompasses the transferable reading, writing and communication skills that support learning across subjects.

International Literacy Association, 2017; Kamil et al., 2008.

Disciplinary Literacy

Disciplinary literacy refers to the specialised ways of reading, reasoning, evaluating evidence and communicating knowledge within a particular subject.

Moje, 2008; Shanahan & Shanahan, 2008, 2012.
